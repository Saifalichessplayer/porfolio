Hyderabad

A city of pearls, palaces, beautiful old streets, and some of the most flavorful food in the country.

Explore the city

Why people love Hyderabad

Food

Hyderabadi biryani, haleem, and Irani chai make the city famous across India.

History

From the Nizams to the Charminar, the city carries a rich and royal past.

Culture

Languages, music, traditions, and lively bazaars give Hyderabad a vibrant identity.

Quick facts

  • Hyderabad is the capital of Telangana in southern India.
  • It is known as the City of Pearls and the City of Nizams.
  • The city combines heritage architecture, business hubs, and food culture.
