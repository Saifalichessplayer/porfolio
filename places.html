Top Places to Visit

Charminar

The iconic monument and surrounding area are famous for old architecture, markets, and local shopping.

Golconda Fort

Explore the grand fort walls, royal halls, and sunset views that highlight the city’s historic glory.

Hussain Sagar Lake

Enjoy a relaxing evening by the lake and admire the city’s skyline with the famous Buddha statue in sight.

Favorite experiences

  • Shop at Laad Bazaar for bangles, pearls, and traditional crafts.
  • Visit a heritage restaurant for Hyderabadi biryani and local sweets.
  • Take an evening walk around the old city to enjoy its colors and atmosphere.
